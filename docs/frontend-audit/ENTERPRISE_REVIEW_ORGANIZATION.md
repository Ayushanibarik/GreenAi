## 4. Evaluative Design & Engineering Benchmarks

### 4.1 Apple Human Interface Guidelines (Scientific Precision)
- **Calm, High-Information Surfaces**: Subdued obsidian/porcelain neutral backgrounds (`#090a0f` / `#f8f9fb`) preventing visual fatigue during 8+ hour research sessions.
- **Optical Typography**: Crisp proportional layout (Inter/system-ui) paired with strict tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for numeric data streams, preventing layout jitter during 10Hz updates.
- **Restraint Over Decoration**: Elimination of decorative gradients, neon border glows, and arbitrary drop-shadows. Borders are subtle 1px structural dividers (`border-subtle`).

### 4.2 Google DeepMind / Material Scientific Workbench
- **Instrument-Grade Telemetry**: Clear visual hierarchy where key scalar numbers (e.g., `42.5 W`, `184.2 gCO₂e`) have prominent, unambiguous weight.
- **Categorical & Semantic Color Restraint**: Colors convey strict functional status (Emerald for high efficiency/grid greenness, Amber for moderate intensity, Rose for power spikes/alert thresholds, Cyan for inference/network streams). No color used purely for visual amusement.

### 4.3 Scientific Data Integrity Standards
- **Green Software Foundation SCI (Software Carbon Intensity)**: Operational emissions calculated with explicit formulas ($E \times I + M$), distinguishing average regional grid intensity from marginal emissions.
- **Telemetry Provenance**: Transparent labeling of data source: `MEASURED` (hardware physical shunt), `ESTIMATED` (CPU/GPU RAPL model), or `SIMULATED` (laboratory test generator).
