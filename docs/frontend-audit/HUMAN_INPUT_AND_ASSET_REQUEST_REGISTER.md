## 1. Executive Summary

While the autonomous enterprise engineering team can systematically refactor the codebase, refine design tokens, eliminate technical debt, and enforce strict WCAG accessibility standards, certain product capabilities require external assets, hardware telemetry connections, or domain credentials from the human Founder / Engineering Team.

This register formally catalogs all external inputs that could elevate GREEN AI from a self-contained research prototype into an industry-leading production software product.
